# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's home, account, or device
  information.
- Accept feedback in good faith and acknowledge mistakes.
- Help keep discussions relevant and accessible to an international community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith argument
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional community

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this policy. They may temporarily or permanently ban
participants for harmful behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through a private contact
method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive or
personal details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical.
